Statistics books with USGS authors

Author

C. Tong

I have attempted to compile a list of statistics-related books with at least one USGS-affiliated author, that I am aware of. The USGS author’s name is indicated in bold. I have listed only the most recent edition I know about. Please let me know of any omissions.

Statistical methods in minerology

Donald. A. Singer and W. David Menzie, 2010: Quantitative Mineral Resource Assessments: An Integrated Approach. Oxford University Press.

Statistical methods in hydrology

Dennis R. Helsel, Robert M. Hirsch, Karen R. Ryberg, Stacey A. Archfield, and Edward J. Gilroy, 2020: Statistical Methods in Water Resources, third edition. Techniques and Methods 4-A3. (Book 4, Hydrologic Analysis and Interpretation, Section A, Statistical Analysis, Chapter 3.) Reston, VA: U.S. Geological Survey.

Ecological statistics

Steven C. Amstrup, Trent L. McDonald, and Brian F. J. Manly (eds.), 2005: Handbook of Capture-Recapture Analysis. Princeton University Press.

Melvin B. Hooten, Devin S. Johnson, Brett T. McClintock, and Juan M. Morales, 2017: Animal Movement: Statistical Models for Telemetry Data. Chapman & Hall/CRC Press.

Marc Kéry and J. Andrew Royle, 2016: Applied Hierarchical Modeling in Ecology: Analysis of Distribution, Abundance and Species Richness in R and BUGS, volume 1: Prelude and Static Models. Academic Press.

Marc Kéry and J. Andrew Royle, 2021: Applied Hierarchical Modeling in Ecology: Analysis of Distribution, Abundance and Species Richness in R and BUGS, volume 2: Dynamic and Advanced Models. Academic Press.

Daryl I. MacKenzie, James D. Nichols, J. Andrew Royle, Kenneth H. Pollock, Larissa L. Bailey, and James E. Hines, 2017: Occupancy Estimation and Modeling: Inferring Patterns and Dynamics of Species Occurrence, second edition. Academic Press.

J. Andrew Royle and Robert M. Dorazio, 2008: Hierarchical Modeling and Inference in Ecology: The Analysis of Data from Populations, Metapopulations and Communities. Academic Press.

J. Andrew Royle, Richard B. Chandler, Rahel Sollmann, and Beth Gardner, 2014: Spatial Capture-Recapture. Academic Press.

Byron K. Williams, James D. Nichols, and Michael J. Conroy, 2002: Analysis and Management of Animal Populations: Modeling, Estimation, and Decision Making. Academic Press.

Censored data

Dennis R. Helsel, 2012: Statistics for Censored Environmental Data Using Minitab and R, second edition. Hoboken, NJ: Wiley.

  • The first edition was titled Nondetects and Data Analysis: Statistics for Censored Environmental Data (Wiley, 2005).

Model selection

Kenneth P. Burnham and David R. Anderson, 2002: Model Selection and Multimodel Inference: A Practical Information-Theoretic Approach, second edition. Springer.

Structural equation models

James B. Grace, 2006: Structural Equation Modeling and Natural Systems. Cambridge University Press.

Bruce H. Pugesek, Adrian Tomer, and Alexander von Eye (eds.), 2003: Structural Equation Modeling: Applications in Ecological and Evolutionary Biology. Cambridge University Press.

Bayesian statistics

Therese M. Donovan and Ruth M. Mickey, 2019: Bayesian Statistics for Beginners: A Step-by-Step Approach. Oxford University Press.

N. Thompson Hobbs and Melvin B. Hooten, 2025: Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists, second edition. Princeton University Press.

Melvin B. Hooten and Trevor Hefley, 2019: Bringing Bayesian Models to Life. Chapman & Hall/CRC Press.

William A. Link and Richard J. Barber, 2009: Bayesian Inference with Ecological Applications. Academic Press.

Song S. Qian, Mark R. DuFour, and Ibrahim Alameddine, 2022: Bayesian Applications in Environmental and Ecological Studies with R and Stan. Chapman & Hall/CRC Press.

Sports analytics

Eric A. Eager and Richard A. Erickson, 2023: Football Analytics with Python and R: Learning Data Science Through the Lens of Sports. O’Reilly.

  • Published in the author’s personal capacity.
